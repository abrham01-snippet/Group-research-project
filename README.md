# E-Waste Management Research – AAU

## 1. Project Overview

**Title:** Evaluating E-Waste Management Practices and Awareness Among ICT Personnel in Addis Ababa University.

This study evaluates e-waste awareness, current management practices, and institutional challenges among ICT personnel at Addis Ababa University (AAU). It uses a descriptive cross-sectional mixed-methods approach to develop recommendations for sustainable Green ICT practices.
